Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a pond?

The construction time depends on the size and complexity of the system. On average, it takes 2-4 weeks for a medium-scale pond.

Is there a warranty for the ponds built?

Yes, we provide a 1-year warranty for the structural components of the ponds and a 6-month warranty for the mechanical systems, along with free maintenance.

What is the monthly operational cost?

Operational costs vary depending on pond size and production level. On average, it's 10-15% of the generated revenue.

Are there payment installment options available?

Yes, we offer various installment schemes with competitive interest rates. Please contact our sales team for more details.
